2. India's Landslide Exposure
GSI estimates that 4.2 lakh km² of India's hilly and mountainous terrain is vulnerable to landslide hazards. This spans 19 States/UTs and 179 districts, with 87,474 inventoried active landslides.
The North-East is particularly important for SANKET. GSI identifies approximately 0.18 million km² of vulnerable terrain in the North-East Himalaya region.

3. Research Strongly Validates the Technology
ML prediction
A 2026 study in Dibang Valley, Arunachal Pradesh used XGBoost and LightGBM for landslide susceptibility modelling. The models achieved approximately AUC ≈ 0.96 and used factors including rainfall, slope, elevation, soil moisture, lithology, NDVI, lineament density, distance to roads and land use/land cover.
NASA LHASA benchmark
NASA's LHASA (Landslide Hazard Assessment for Situational Awareness) combines GPM rainfall, SMAP soil moisture, terrain/geological information and machine learning to generate near-real-time landslide hazard information. The system operates at approximately 1 km × 1 km resolution.
4. SANKET's Research and Product Gap
The defensible claim is not that nobody has built an AI landslide prediction system. Existing research already demonstrates strong capabilities in landslide susceptibility modelling, satellite monitoring and rainfall-based nowcasting.
The opportunity is integration: connecting prediction with real-world visual evidence, exposure analysis and last-mile field response in one localized operational platform.
Predict → Observe → Fuse → Prioritise → Act → Learn
5. SANKET's Core Differentiator: Risk Fusion
Environmental intelligence: rainfall + soil moisture + terrain + historical landslides
Predictive intelligence: XGBoost / ML
Visual intelligence: satellite change + YOLO + field photographs + videos
Risk fusion: combine independent signals into one location-specific assessment
Decision intelligence: GIS + road/village exposure + emergency prioritisation
Action: alerts + field verification + response
SANKET aims to answer not only 'Where is landslide hazard likely?' but also 'What evidence confirms it, who or what is exposed, and what should happen next?'

8. SANKET Mountain Safety / TrekSafe
For commercial operators, the full government dashboard can be packaged as a focused route-safety product.
Before a trek
Route + dates + camps + GPS trail
Historical landslide susceptibility
Rainfall and weather forecast
Soil-moisture and terrain conditions
Recent satellite changes
Known incidents and vulnerable route sections
Alternative-route assessment
During a trek
A guide can capture a geo-tagged photo/video of a crack, debris field, rockfall or damaged trail. The app records GPS and timestamp, while YOLO/CV analyses visible evidence.
Example outputs: Debris 94%; Rockfall 89%; Trail obstruction 93%. The system can then generate a route-hazard warning and recommend field verification.

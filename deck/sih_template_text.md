# Proposed Solution:
AI-based system to recommend the most suitable crop.
Inputs: soil parameters (N, P, K, pH, organic carbon) + weather conditions. Integrate real-time weather & market price APIs.
Suggest alternate crops to reduce failure risk. Predict yield, profitability, and give decision insights. Provide farmer-friendly mobile app.
Low-cost, scalable, and easy to adopt in rural India. Suggest actionable steps (e.g., fertilizer use, pH balance).
